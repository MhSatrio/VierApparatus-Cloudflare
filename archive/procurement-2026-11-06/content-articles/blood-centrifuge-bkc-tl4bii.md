## Where this type of centrifuge fits

Plasma and serum separation is a routine step in clinical laboratories, blood-collection and screening workflows, and many research settings that handle blood samples. A benchtop, low-speed unit is typically chosen for this kind of routine separation rather than for high-speed work such as pelleting small particles. Whether the BKC-TL4BII is the right fit depends on your tubes, your volumes and your protocol, which is why the checklist below matters.

## Specifications to confirm before you buy

Ask the supplier, through us or directly, to confirm each of the following in writing:

1. **Speed and relative centrifugal force (RCF).** Protocols are often written in *g*-force, not rpm. Confirm the maximum speed, the maximum RCF and the rotor radius so you can convert correctly.
2. **Capacity and tube compatibility.** How many tubes per run, which tube sizes and types (for example blood-collection tubes), and which adapters are included.
3. **Rotor options.** Whether the rotor is fixed-angle or swing-out, what is included, and what optional rotors exist.
4. **Timer and speed control.** Run-time range, ramp-up and braking behavior, and whether settings can be saved.
5. **Safety features.** How the lid lock works, imbalance protection if any, and any applicable safety certification the supplier can document.
6. **Electrical requirements.** Voltage, frequency and plug type for your country. Confirm this early, because it is a common cause of delay on imported equipment.
7. **Noise and footprint.** Dimensions, weight and stated noise level, so you can check bench space and the working environment.
8. **Warranty, spare parts and service.** Warranty length, what it covers, how spare parts are supplied and who provides support in your country.
9. **Documentation.** Datasheet, user manual, and any test or conformity documents you need for your institution or for import.

## How to prepare a clear quotation request

A request that is specific gets a faster and more comparable answer. Include:

- The exact model, **BKC-TL4BII**, and the quantity you need.
- The tubes and sample volumes you will run.
- Your delivery country and the local voltage and plug type.
- Any required documents (datasheet, certificates, manual language).
- Whether you want installation guidance, spare parts or accessories quoted at the same time.
- Your target timeline.
