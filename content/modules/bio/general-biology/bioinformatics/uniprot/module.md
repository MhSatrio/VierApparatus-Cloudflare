UniProt is the reference resource for protein sequences and their functional annotation. The UniProt Consortium (2023) describes the Knowledgebase as the combination of a curated section and a large automatically generated section, and most protein analyses begin by finding the right entry in it. This topic is the practical guide to that task: how to find an entry, how to read it, how to choose between its versions, and how to convert the identifiers of a dataset so that it can be combined with other resources.

The protein database topic in the biological databases level introduced the resource. This topic deals with the work of using it. The chapters cover the record and its sections, the search syntax, the annotation and its evidence, the isoforms and variants of each protein, and the identifier mapping that connects UniProt to the other resources of the field.

Proteins are the products of genes, and a UniProt entry is the place where a gene's product is described. Alberts et al. (2022) describe the molecular roles of proteins that the entries record, and each chapter ties a practical step to the biology it reports.
