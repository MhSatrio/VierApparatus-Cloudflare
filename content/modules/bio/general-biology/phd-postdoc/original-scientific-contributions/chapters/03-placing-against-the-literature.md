A contribution is original only in relation to what came before it, and the researcher must place it honestly against the literature. A systematic review of the existing evidence establishes what is already known, and it reveals which prior results the new work confirms, extends, qualifies or contradicts. Ioannidis (2005) described how the failure to engage with prior evidence leads to the repeated rediscovery of known results, and the placing of the contribution is the remedy.

Prior results may be confirmed, extended, qualified or contradicted by the new work, and each relation should be stated explicitly. A confirmation strengthens the evidence for a result, an extension adds to its scope, a qualification limits it, and a contradiction demands an explanation. Cumming (2014) argues that the comparison should be made on the estimates and their intervals, since two results that disagree only within their intervals do not truly contradict each other.

Earlier work may have anticipated the contribution, and the honest researcher acknowledges it. A claim to have discovered something that was previously reported, even in a less visible form, damages the researcher's credibility and wrongs the earlier investigators. Munafò et al. (2017) argued that the attribution of prior work is a basic obligation of research, and it applies to the reporting of new results as much as to the citation of method.

The comparison with previous estimates should be made quantitatively, with each estimate given with its interval and the combined evidence reported where the studies are comparable. Peng (2011) argued that the basis of each comparison, including the studies chosen and the reasons for their inclusion, must be documented so that the placing can be checked.

Claims of priority must be supported by the literature, and they should be made cautiously. The report should state how the contribution relates to each important prior result, so that the reader can see its novelty in context and judge the claim for themselves. Ioannidis (2005) emphasised that honest placing protects the field from the inflation of its own history.
