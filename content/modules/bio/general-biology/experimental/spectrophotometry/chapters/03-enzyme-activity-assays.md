An enzyme assay measures the rate at which a reaction proceeds, and in spectrophotometry the rate is followed by a change in absorbance. The reduction of NAD+ to NADH, which absorbs at 340 nanometres, is a common signal, and the assay of a dehydrogenase can be followed continuously in a cuvette. Where the reaction does not produce a chromophore, the product is coupled to a second enzyme that produces one, so that the rate of the first reaction is measured through the second.

The rate must be measured under conditions in which it is proportional to the enzyme concentration. This means working with the initial linear phase of the reaction, before substrate is depleted or product inhibits the enzyme, and with substrate in excess of its Michaelis constant, as the kinetic analysis in the Biochemistry module describes. The coupling enzyme must not be rate-limiting, which requires that it be supplied in excess and that its activity be checked separately.

Controls establish that the change is enzymatic. A reaction without the enzyme should show no change, and a reaction with a heat-inactivated enzyme should also show none. Changes in absorbance that occur without the enzyme, from the spontaneous decay of the substrate or from turbidity, must be subtracted. The buffer, temperature and pH must be kept constant, since enzyme activity depends on each.

Endpoint assays stop the reaction at a fixed time and measure the product. They are suited to samples that cannot be followed continuously, but the stopping method must be complete and reproducible, and the measurement must lie in the linear range of the product.

Ioannidis (2005) described how results depend on choices made in analysis, and the rate calculation, including the range of time used and the criteria for linearity, should be fixed before the assay is run.

Alberts et al. (2022) describe the enzymes of metabolism that these assays measure. The protocol should record the substrate concentration, the enzyme amount, the wavelength, the temperature and the controls, since the rate is meaningful only with these.
