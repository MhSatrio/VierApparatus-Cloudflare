The ethical obligations of an investigator continue throughout a study, and a study that is well designed at the start can become harmful during its conduct. Monitoring allows investigators and committees to see whether participants are being harmed, whether the study is delivering the information it promised and whether it should continue. For studies with serious risks, an independent data safety monitoring board examines the accumulating data at pre-specified points and advises on whether the study should proceed.

Stopping rules are defined in advance and state the circumstances in which a study must be halted. A rule for harm states the level of adverse effect that triggers a halt, and a rule for futility states the point at which the study is unlikely to answer its question. Ioannidis (2005) described how studies that continue without a clear stopping rule are vulnerable to the influence of accumulating results, and the pre-specification protects the integrity of the decision.

Adverse events must be reported and assessed for their relation to the study. The assessment asks whether the event was caused by the intervention, could have been caused by it or was unrelated, and the judgement must be recorded with its reasons. Cumming (2014) argues that the risk should be reported with its uncertainty, since a small number of events gives a wide interval and a reassuring result may simply reflect a small study.

Stopping for efficacy, when a treatment appears clearly beneficial, is more difficult to justify than stopping for harm. An early stop for benefit tends to overestimate the effect, because the chance fluctuations that led to the stop are retained in the estimate. Munafò et al. (2017) argued that the reporting of stopped studies must be complete, and their results should be published so that the evidence is not lost. The decision to stop, and the reasons for it, should be communicated to participants, to the committee and to those affected, and the study's results should be reported whether it continued to its planned end or not.
