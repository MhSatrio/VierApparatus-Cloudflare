Crop gene banks hold seeds of many varieties and of the wild relatives of crops, and they are the reservoir of the genetic diversity on which future breeding will depend. Godfray et al. (2010) emphasised that the conservation of agricultural biodiversity is part of the response to food security, since a narrow genetic base is vulnerable to new pests and changes in climate. The seeds are conserved at low temperature and humidity, which keeps them viable for decades.

Genomic characterisation of a collection reveals its diversity, including the duplicates that add little new variation. A collection may contain many accessions that are genetically almost identical, and their redundancy can be removed without losing diversity. The characterisation uses genome-wide markers, and the number of markers and the sampling of the collection affect the conclusions drawn about its structure.

Wild relatives of crops carry resistance and tolerance traits that have been lost from the cultivated varieties during domestication and breeding. The genetic bottlenecks of domestication narrowed the diversity of many crops, and the reintroduction of wild variation through crosses is one way to restore it. The wild relatives are themselves at risk, and their conservation is an additional responsibility of gene banks.

A core collection is a subset of a large collection chosen to represent the diversity of the whole, so that it can be studied in detail with limited resources. The selection of a core collection requires a genomic or phenotypic summary of the full collection, and its representativeness should be tested. Regeneration of accessions, which grows the seeds to produce new stock, can lose diversity if the population is small or if the plants are not allowed to cross freely, and the procedure must be designed to limit this loss. Cumming (2014) argues that the diversity of a collection should be reported with its interval, and Ioannidis (2005) described how a core collection may fail to represent the rare variation that matters for a particular trait.
